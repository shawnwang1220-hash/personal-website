## Building a Global B2B Digital Marketing System from Zero

For many traditional companies going global, the first step isn't running ads — it's standing up the **demand generation infrastructure**.

### Four core modules
1. **Website & landing pages**: where traffic lands and converts; also the starting point of tracking
2. **Ad platforms**: Google Ads + LinkedIn Ads together cover search intent and the buying committee
3. **Marketing stack**: GA4, GTM and form tools (e.g. Zoho Forms) wired into one data flow
4. **Lead management**: routing and nurturing from form fill to CRM

### A minimum viable data flow
Site tracking → GTM collection → GA4 aggregation → back to ad platforms → CRM follow-up

> Without a closed data loop, a bigger budget just buys more impressions.

Get this foundation right, and every later optimization has something to stand on.
