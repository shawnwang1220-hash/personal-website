## Building a Lead Nurture Automation in Pardot

A lead isn't something you hand to sales the moment it arrives — it needs to be **nurtured** to readiness, with the right content at the right time.

### Flow skeleton
1. **Capture**: landing page form → writes a Prospect
2. **Score**: accumulate points from behaviour (opens, clicks, pricing page visits)
3. **Qualify**: crossing the threshold makes it an MQL, auto-routed to the right rep
4. **Engage**: automated email sequences keep nurturing leads below the bar

### Why it's worth it
- Sales only picks up "warm" leads, which convert better
- Marketing can quantify each piece's contribution
- Marketing and Sales end up speaking one language

> Automation isn't about doing less — it's about putting people where their effort counts.
